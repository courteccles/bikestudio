# S-SHELL — Studio Shell and Side View

**Status:** approved 2026-11-04. **Source:** plan section 1 (`bike-application`, user interface), section 3 (architecture), section 5 (display preferences, themes), design principles 6 and 7, D13, D20, D25 in `../decisions.md`, S-UNITS U3 and U13, S-COMPONENTS C12 and C13. Mockups: `../designer experience images/Specification/`.

## Purpose

The first runnable app. A designer opens bikestudio, creates or opens a `.bikestud` project, sets wheels, travel and geometry in the Specification panel, and sees the bike in the side view, redrawn from `Solved` on every edit, with the diagnostics listed underneath. Undo and redo come with S-LINK-EDITOR, and picking components with a later Components tab.

## Decisions

| # | Decision | Status |

| H1 | **Crate:** a new `bike-application` crate, the only one that knows about a user interface (plan section 1), with the binary `bikestudio`. It depends on `gpui-kit`. The plan says `0.8`; crates.io now has `0.9.0` (2026-11-02), so pin `0.9` unless it fails on Linux or macOS. `resolve` stays in `bike-components` (C12); the app calls it. | Confirmed |
| H2 | **Command and reducer** (plan section 3): the interface sends `Command`s: `SetWheel`, `SetTravel`, `PinGeometry`, `FreeGeometry`, `SetFork`, `SetCrank`, and `SetTubes`. `PickComponent` comes with the Components tab. A reducer applies each one to the `Project` and raises a generation counter. The pipeline (`resolve`, `solve_geometry_with`, `solve_tubes_with`) runs again only when the counter changes. No undo stack yet. Changing components will never be an undo step (S-LINK-EDITOR). | Confirmed |
| H3 | **Layout** (mockup `04`, without the **Next: Linkage** button, which isn't built yet): a top bar, the side view, the Specification panel on the right, a bottom tray, and a status bar. The top bar holds the **Bikes** menu, the project name with a saved/unsaved dot, the **Specification · Linkage · Analysis** mode switch, the settings button and the wordmark. Linkage and Analysis are shown but disabled until S-LINK-EDITOR and S-KINEMATICS. The panels are `gpui-component` `dock` and `resizable`. | Confirmed |
| H4 | **Specification panel** (mockups `04`–`12`): **Wheels** front and rear as a segmented choice of the `wheel_size.toml` keys. **Travel** front and rear, always in millimetres (U12). **Geometry**: one row per S-GEOM variable, each with a lock (pinned or free) and a value field. A pinned field holds the designer's value. A free field shows the solved value, dimmed, and a value from the defaults is labelled as such. Typing into a free field pins it. Fields use the S-UNITS parser and the no-drift rule (U7). | Confirmed |
| H5 | **Both seat angles:** the mockups show one "Seat angle" row, but S-GEOM has two independent ones (G4). The panel shows **Effective seat angle** and **Actual seat angle** as separate rows, each pinnable. The effective angle is what the published charts give and all the chart calculations need. | Confirmed |
| H6 | **Frame section** in the panel, below Geometry: material, seat tube length (lockable like geometry) and the three junction offsets (S-TUBES). Per-tube sections go in a collapsed "Tubes" group. Crank length and pedal size go in a "Cranks" group. | Confirmed |
| H7 | **Side view:** a GPUI `canvas` drawn from `Solved` alone: ground line, wheels at their diameters, the fork from its S-COMPONENTS drawing (C13), the frame tubes as centrelines with their diameters, the drive unit outline when one is fitted, and the crank centre marked "0,0 crank axis". The wheelbase is dimensioned. A focused geometry field shows its dimension callout (mockup `07`). For a hardtail, the at-sag and full-travel states can be shown as ghost overlays. Pan with the middle mouse button or space-drag, zoom with the wheel, and **Fit** frames the bike. | Confirmed |
| H8 | **Always drawable** (design principle 6): with nothing pinned, the view draws the default bike and labels it "Side view · preview from defaults", as in the mockups. When the solve fails, the last good drawing stays up, dimmed, with the errors in the tray. | Confirmed |
| H9 | **Bottom tray tabs:** **Diagnostics**, **Curves**, **Feel** and **Assistant**. Diagnostics lists every diagnostic with its severity icon, code and message. Curves, Feel and Assistant show "Coming soon" until S-KINEMATICS, S-FEEL and the post-version 1 assistant (D12) fill them. There's no History tab. Components will be picked from a **Components** tab, which comes in a later spec, not S-SHELL. | Confirmed |
| H10 | **Files** (mockups `01`–`03`, `13`): the Bikes menu has New project (Ctrl+N), Open (Ctrl+O), Change folder (default `~/Bikes`), the five most recent projects, Save (Ctrl+S) and Save as (Ctrl+Shift+S). macOS uses Cmd. New opens `Project::default()` (D20), named `untitled`. Closing the window or opening another project with unsaved changes asks Save, Don't save or Cancel. There's no autosave. Files use `.bikestud` (D25), and a load error shows `LoadError`'s message. | Confirmed |
| H11 | **App settings** (mockup `14`), stored in the platform config directory, never in a project: theme (Dark or Drafting) and the projects folder. The display unit toggle stays in the status bar (U13). The mockup's Assistant section is left out (D12). | Confirmed |
| H12 | **Themes:** Dark ships in S-SHELL. The Drafting theme (plan section 5) draws the same scene with different rendering, so the scene is built once as plain shapes with a style role (frame, wheel, dimension, centreline), and the theme maps roles to strokes and fills. Drafting itself comes in a later spec. | Confirmed |
| H13 | **No frame budget benchmark yet.** Performance is optimised later; S-SHELL adds no benchmark or CI timing check. | Confirmed |
| H14 | **Testing without a window:** the reducer, the panel's view model (what each field shows and whether it's dimmed, pinned or defaulted) and the scene builder are plain Rust and unit tested. GPUI tests (`gpui::TestAppContext`) cover opening, editing and saving a project. CI builds the app on Linux and macOS. | Confirmed |
| H15 | **No new diagnostic codes.** The mockups' `geometry.unpinned`, `suspension.none`, `rider.default` and `linkage.empty` aren't added until it's clear they're needed; the tray shows the codes the solvers already produce. | Confirmed |

## Scope

1. **`bike-application`:** `Command`, reducer, app state (project, path, dirty flag, generation, `Solved`), scene builder, views (top bar, side view, Specification panel, tray, status bar), settings file and file dialogs.
2. **`bike-model`:** nothing new is expected; `Solved` gains `components: ResolvedComponents` if the side view needs it.
3. **CI:** build `bike-application` on both systems. On Linux, CI installs GPUI's system packages.
4. **Out of scope:** undo and redo (S-LINK-EDITOR), picking components (the Components tab), the Linkage and Analysis modes, the contents of the Curves, Feel and Assistant tabs, the Drafting theme (H12), exports (S-EXPORT) and clearance highlighting (S-CLEARANCE).

## Done when

- `cargo run -p bike-application` opens an empty project on Linux and macOS showing the default preview.
- Pinning each geometry value redraws the side view, and the free values update.
- New, Open, Save and Save as round-trip a `.bikestud` file, and unsaved changes prompt before they're lost.
- CI builds and tests `bike-application` on Linux and macOS.

## Changes after approval

2026-11-04, from the first look at the running shell:

- H3 layout: the grid and drawing fill the whole shell between the top bar and status bar. The panel and tray are cards floating over it. The top bar and status bar have no dividing lines. Fit frames the bike in the space the cards leave.
- H4 panel: Cranks is a normal section, always shown. The Geometry section runs in letter order and adds fork axle-to-crown and offset (editable, via `SetFork`) and front centre, rear centre and standover (read only).
- H7 side view: every value with a letter has its dimension drawn, labelled with the letter; the focused field also shows its value. The letters follow the designer's geometry drawing: A reach, B stack, D head angle, E actual seat angle, F effective seat angle, G front centre, H rear centre, I wheelbase, J BB height, K standover, L seat tube length, M fork offset, N head tube length, O axle-to-crown. C (effective top tube) and P aren't used. Q BB drop and R chainstay are added. Standover is the top of the top tube above the ground at the tube's midpoint.
- H7: dotted extension lines run from the measured points out to each offset dimension, so it is clear where it lines up.
- H8: no "Preview from defaults" caption. A drawing that can't solve is still shown dimmed.
- H9: Material and the tube fields are in a Tubes tab.
- H11: there's no settings dialog yet.
- Defaults aren't diagnostics. Wheels always show a selected size, the default when none is set.
- H7: tyres are drawn without knobs.
- H3: the Specification / Linkage / Analysis mode bar is centred in the top bar.
- Segmented controls: the mode bar, the wheel size selectors and the tray tabs share one style.
- H4, H9: a divider line (`#262626`) runs under the Specification heading and under the tray tabs, edge to edge across the card.
- Colours follow the mockups: shell, side view, cards, segmented strips, inputs and popup menus use the sampled mockup palette, and the component theme is pointed at it so menus and buttons match. - The status bar is 40 px tall (was 28), giving the status and units more room above and below.
- H3 layout: the side view runs to the right edge of the shell, open on that side. The panel and tray sit 16 px inside it on every side, including the right, with 16 px between them.
- H4 panel: Seat tube length (L) moves into Geometry, in letter order. The Frame section is removed, with its tube junction and seat tube overhang rows; Cranks stays.
- H7 Re-center (was Fit): a button over the ground, just above the right end of the tray, that resets pan and zoom and frames the bike as it is now. It is enabled only when the view isn't already centred. Between re-centres the view keeps its framing: edits such as a wheel size redraw the bike in place, never changing the zoom, so the grid and crank axis stay still.
- H4 fields: clicking or tabbing into a field selects its whole value, so typing replaces it. Up and Down arrows step the value (0.1° for angles, 1 mm or 0.1 in for lengths; Shift steps ten times as far), commit it at once (pinning a free field, as typing does) and keep it selected. Read-only rows ignore the arrows.
- H7 grid: 1 cm minor and 10 cm major lines, as built. Below the ground the lines are dimmer (`#0E1012`), as in mockup 04.
- H3: the side view's left edge lines up with the Bikes dropdown (32 px). The Bikes dropdown has a caret, and a settings gear sits before the wordmark; it does nothing until there's a settings dialog (H11). The app registers the bundled icon assets so icons draw.
- H7 ground: below the ground line the view is filled darker, with the grid running on over it and a 20 px band of fine (0.5 px) diagonal hatching under the line, as in mockup 04.
- H4 panel: the Geometry section no longer runs in letter order. Its order is Reach, Stack, Head angle, Head tube length, Effective seat angle, Seat tube angle, Seat tube length, Standover, Front center, Rear center, Chainstay, Wheelbase, BB drop, BB height, Fork offset, Axle-to-crown. Each row keeps its letter. Three labels are renamed: Actual seat angle is now Seat tube angle, Front centre and Rear centre are now Front center and Rear center, and Fork axle-to-crown is now Axle-to-crown.

Each box has a header (Head tube, Saddle, Bottom bracket, Fork) and the same 8 px of padding on every side: rows inside a box are as tall as their 24 px inputs, 8 px apart, and the header has no leading. Labels and inputs inside a box stay in line with the rows outside it.
- Contrast: the mockup colours made outlines hard to see, so they are raised. Card borders (panel, tray, side view, inputs, dropdowns) are `#45484C` (was `#232527`), dividers and group boxes `#3A3C3F` (was `#262626`), and segmented strips `#202326` (was `#191B1D`) with a card-border outline.
- 2026-11-05 (S-EDIT-CENTERS E3, E5): front center (G), rear center (H) and standover (K) are editable rows with locks, no longer read only. Seat tube overhang is a new editable row in the Saddle box after seat tube length, lettered **S**. Its dimension runs along the seat tube from the top tube junction to the seat tube top, on the opposite side from seat tube length (L). Geometry order: ... Seat tube length, Seat tube overhang, Standover, Front center, ...
- 2026-11-05: group box headers use the field label size. Seat tube overhang is labelled **Mast**. The side view draws a dotted line from the underside of the down pedal to the ground, labelled with the pedal ground clearance (G13).
- 2026-11-05: dimensions move off the bike where they can. Front center (G) and rear center (H) are one row below the ground, split at the crank, with wheelbase (I) under them. Stack (B) is ahead of the front wheel. Chainstay (R) is offset below the stay. BB height (J) is behind the down pedal, and BB drop (Q) is ahead of the forward pedal, carried from the front axle.
- 2026-11-05: the side view draws a saddle centred on the saddle point, the effective seat line (crank centre to saddle point) as a thick dotted line, and a dotted line from the head tube top back to the saddle point at stack height (G4). The seat angle callouts move off that line: E (seat tube angle) sits on the seat tube axis above the seat tube top, and F (effective) sits part way up the effective line. Each has a horizontal reference running back, with the label past its end.
- 2026-11-05, revised: the saddle drawing is replaced by a small circle at the saddle point. E and F are arcs centred on the saddle point, each sweeping from its seat line up to the head tube horizontal (F inside, E outside), labelled just inside the arc at the usual label gap. D (head angle) is drawn the same way, on the same horizontal: an arc at the head tube top from the steering axis, ahead and down, up to the horizontal. The saddle point is a solid dot in the dimension colour.
- 2026-11-05: tube ends are drawn mitred to the tubes they join. Ends meeting the head tube (top and down tubes) or the seat tube (top tube and seatstays) are drawn as coped round-tube joints. Seen side on, a tube of radius r meeting a tube of radius R is cut at sqrt(R² − r² + s²) from R's axis, where s is the distance across the joining tube. So the cut meets R's edge at the sides and curves in toward its axis in the middle. The down tube is also coped to the seat tube at the BB. Each line across it is cut to whichever it reaches first, the seat tube or the shell, and a cut only counts where the joined tube actually is along its length. The chainstays are cut to the BB shell's outline, a concave arc. A joining tube larger than the tube it meets (for example a top tube over a thinner seat tube) gets a cope scaled to stay a smooth curve, at most 0.9 of the joined tube's radius deep. Other ends stay square: the seat tube, rear dropouts, and ends on a drive unit outline. This is drawing only; tube lengths and masses still use the centrelines. Tube centrelines are no longer drawn; the seat tube axis up to the saddle point still is. BB drop (Q) hangs from the middle of a dotted line between the axles down to crank height. Dashed lines have dashes twice as long and are slightly thicker.
- 2026-11-06: an edit that would turn a model that solves into one with no geometry or no tubes (a cleared, freed or unlocked value whose default clashes with the rest) is refused before it's applied: the field keeps its value, is outlined red, and an `edit.refused` error, "Invalid geometry", shows in the diagnostics until the next command.


## Open questions

- None.
- 2026-11-06 (H4): clearing a field and leaving it changes nothing; the field shows its most recent value again. A value is freed with its lock. Rows without a lock (tube sections, crank, pedal and fork overrides, travel) have no way back to the default yet.
- 2026-11-06: the side view draws a rear dropout on rigid frames and hardtails: the convex hull of a 20 mm circle round the rear axle (concentric with the hub) and a tab 50 mm up each stay, as wide as the stay, with a 12 mm thru-axle on top. The stays and dropout are one piece, drawn as a union (`Shape::Union`): every outline at twice the hairline, then every fill over them, opaque, so only the outer outline shows, at any angle between the stays. Drawing-only placeholder sizes; full suspension gets none (S-LINK).
- 2026-11-06: BB drop can be negative, so in its field a leading sign is the value's sign, not a nudge (U7): typing or stepping to "-30" gives −30 mm. Other lengths still nudge.
- 2026-11-06: crank arms are drawn rounded: the hull of an 18 mm circle about the crank centre and a 12 mm circle about the pedal axle (drawing-only placeholder sizes).
- 2026-11-06: the front axle is drawn like the rear (12 mm thru-axle). The fork steerer is drawn from the crown up through the head tube to 30 mm above its top (spacers and stem; drawing-only placeholder), tapering from the bottom to the top diameter over the head tube for a tapered steerer; the head tube is drawn over it.
- 2026-11-06: shape fills are opaque (each fill laid over the view background), so no line shows through a shape. The seat tube axis centreline is gone; a dashed line (the Extension style) runs from the seat tube top to the saddle point instead.
- 2026-11-06: the ghost crank (straight down, unfilled) draws its pedal whole and its arm only outside the pedal (`Shape::Segments`), so no arm edges show inside the pedal.
- 2026-11-06: both pedals show their axle: a 7 mm radius circle (9/16 inch spindle; drawing-only) on top of the pedal.
